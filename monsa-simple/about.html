The philosophy

How we think.

Fit first

We only work with businesses we genuinely believe in. If we don't think paid media can move the needle, we'll tell you before we send a proposal.

No padding

We'd rather say no to a bad-fit client than pad a roster. Small is intentional.

No percent-of-spend

We don't take a percentage of ad spend, because it rewards the wrong behavior.

Written down

We write things down. Every engagement has a thesis and clear KPIs before work starts.

Month-to-month

No annual lock-in. We earn the next month every month. If it isn't working, you walk.

Honest early

If we can't beat what you're doing today, we'll tell you in week two, not month six.
