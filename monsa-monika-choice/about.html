About

Built by someone who ran these platforms from the inside.

Monsa Media is run by Monika Lange, a former YouTube product lead with 8 years at Google, who has spent the years since proving the playbook works outside the building too.

The short version

She's built client accounts from zero and scaled them to seven figures in spend, run rebuilds that lifted CTRs 50%+, and delivered ROAS gains of 60%+ on accounts that already had budget moving.

She founded Monsa for the kind of clients who have been burned by agencies that overpromise and underdeliver — and who want someone who'll be upfront about what will and won't work before any contract is signed.

The longer version

Eight years inside. The years since, on the outside.

Philosophy

How we choose what to say yes to.

Belief first.

We only work with businesses we genuinely believe in. If we don't think paid media can move the needle, we'll tell you before we send a proposal.

No padding.

We'd rather say no to a bad-fit client than pad a roster.

No %-of-spend.

We don't take a percentage of ad spend, because it rewards the wrong behavior.

Write things down.

Every engagement has a written thesis. Clear KPIs from week one.

No lock-in.

Month-to-month, 30-day notice. We earn the next month every month.

Tell early.

If we can't beat what you're doing today, we'll tell you in week two — not month six.
