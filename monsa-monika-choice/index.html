About Monika

Hi, I'm Monika.

Twelve years in marketing. I started in traditional marketing at large FMCGs, learning the craft on brands where the work was about brand equity, distribution, and shelf, before moving into digital.

Eight of those years were at Google, first as an account manager, then a product specialist, and most recently as YouTube product lead for EMEA. Across all of it I worked the full advertiser spectrum, from SMB through enterprise, in finance, retail, automotive, and travel.

Today I run paid media end-to-end for a small list of clients. The work spans Google Ads, Meta Ads, and senior consulting for in-house teams. New builds taken from a standing start to seven-figure spend. ROAS tripled on accounts that were already running.

I founded Monsa to bring that combination of platform-level rigor and operator chops to a small number of clients I genuinely believe in.

FAQ

Things people ask before booking.

Who do you work with?
Businesses with a real offer where I can see paid media moving the needle. Spend size matters less than fit. SaaS, ecommerce, DTC, and lead gen are all common.
Who runs my account?
I'm personally on every engagement, in the strategy and the execution. When more hands are needed I bring in senior specialists I've worked with for years and vetted myself. You'll never be handed off to a junior account manager.
Do you have a minimum spend?
No hard minimum. The conversation is always about fit first, spend second.
Do you handle creative?
I direct creative strategy and brief it, and work with a short list of editors and designers I trust. I don't sell creative as a line item.
Do you do SEO, influencer, TikTok, email?
No. Google and Meta, properly. I'll refer out everything else.
What does it cost?
Every engagement is scoped individually based on objectives, account complexity, and spend. I don't take a percentage of ad spend. Book a call and you'll have a scoped proposal in 48 hours.
Ready when you are

Ready for senior eyes on your account?

Tell me a bit about your business. I'll reply within one working day with an honest take on whether I'm the right fit. If I'm not, I'll say so.
